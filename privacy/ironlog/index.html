IronLog Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026 · Published by Daniel Wagner

IronLog is a workout tracker. Everything you log stays on your phone.

Your workouts

Splits, workouts, exercises, sets, body weight and settings are stored only on your device. The home-screen widget reads a small summary of your next workout from the same device. There is no account, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Importing and exporting

When you import a backup or a CSV from another workout app, IronLog reads the file you pick on your device. When you export, IronLog creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; where it goes from there is up to you.

Analytics and advertising

IronLog shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. If that changes, we will update this policy, and ask for your permission where required, before it happens.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase All Data in the app, or delete the app, to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

IronLog is not directed at children under 13.
