Pixie Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026 · Published by Daniel Wagner

Pixie is a digital closet: you photograph your clothes and accessories, and Pixie catalogues them so you can search, mix and match outfits, and check whether you already own something. This policy explains what that involves.

Your account

Pixie uses an email address and password to sign you in, so your closet is available on any device. Accounts are managed by Supabase, our hosting provider. We never see your password.

Your photos and closet

Photos you add, and the details about each piece (such as name, colour, category, notes, outfits and wear history), are stored in a private database and file storage hosted by Supabase in Canada. Only your signed-in account can access them. We do not sell them or share them with anyone except the services described below.

On supported iPhones, removing the background from a photo happens on your device.

Automatic tagging

When you add a photo, or use "Do I own it?", the photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to describe the item (for example "white high-top sneakers"). Anthropic processes it to return those tags. Under its commercial terms, it does not use this data to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy. Photos from "Do I own it?" are not saved to your closet.

On your device

Pixie keeps small caches on your phone, such as thumbnails, recent searches and your theme setting, so the app stays fast. Free-access codes you redeem are stored only on your device.

Analytics and advertising

Pixie currently shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking. If that changes, we will update this policy, and ask for your permission where required, before it happens.

Deleting your data

Go to Me → Delete account in the app to permanently delete your account, every photo and all closet data. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Pixie is not directed at children under 13.
