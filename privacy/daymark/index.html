Daymark Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026 · Published by Daniel Wagner

Daymark is a mood and activity diary. Everything you write in it stays on your phone.

Your entries

Moods, activities, notes and settings are stored only in a database on your device. There is no account, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see your diary.

Importing and exporting

When you import a backup, Daymark reads the file you pick on your device. When you export, Daymark creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; where it goes from there is up to you.

Analytics and advertising

Daymark shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. If that changes, we will update this policy, and ask for your permission where required, before it happens.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Children

Daymark is not directed at children under 13.
